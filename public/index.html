02

Built around bounded autonomy.

Ghost is not framed as a black-box agent. The core path is deterministic and local-first: recorded behavior becomes compressed steps, compressed steps become reviewable workflow state, and every replay leaves a trace that can be inspected.

Trusted core

Recording, compression, policy checks, approvals, replay, execution history, and audit storage.

Experimental AI

May suggest names, summaries, insights, and future improvements. It does not silently mutate workflows.

Reviewer signal

Concrete UI, local data paths, green CI recovery, and a release process that can be verified end to end.
