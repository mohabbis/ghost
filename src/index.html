Welcome to Ghost

Ghost watches the repetitive things you do on screen, then replays them for you — record a task once, run it forever.

🔒 Ghost Guard blocks sensitive text-field capture and suppresses secret-like keystrokes.

🔑

Grant recording permissions

To watch and replay clicks and keystrokes, macOS needs you to allow Ghost under System Settings → Privacy & Security in both Accessibility and Input Monitoring.

Not granted yet

After you flip the switch, you may need to quit and reopen Ghost — macOS applies Accessibility changes when the app launches.

🛡️

Secure your data

Optionally protect Ghost with a password. Your recorded workflows are then encrypted on this device and Ghost asks for the password on launch.

There is no recovery if you forget it — the password never leaves your device.

You're all set

Three steps to your first automation:

  1. Start Recording, then do your task once.
  2. Hit Stop when you're done.
  3. Press Replay — Ghost repeats it for you.
